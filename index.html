About Me

I am an Agile delivery professional with 14+ years of IT experience, including seven years as a Scrum Master and Agile Delivery Lead across banking, cards, payments and gaming.

My experience spans:

  • Agile transformation and team coaching
  • Multi-squad delivery coordination
  • Project and program-level planning
  • Stakeholder and leadership engagement
  • Risk, dependency and impediment management
  • Delivery governance and continuous improvement
  • AI-enabled reporting and workflow automation

In my Agile Lead role, I coordinate delivery across four squads, aligning priorities, capacity, milestones, risks and cross-team dependencies. I work closely with Product Owners, engineering leaders and stakeholders to strengthen delivery predictability, transparency and team ownership.

My SAFe experience includes supporting PI Planning across three teams, participating in Scrum of Scrums, resolving cross-team dependencies and enabling coordinated delivery at program level.

Flagship Initiatives

Some of the key initiatives I have led or contributed to include:

  • Led Agile Ways of Working workshops across 10 squads, receiving 90% positive feedback
  • Created the Delivery Disruptors framework to identify recurring systemic impediments and guide focused resolution
  • Built more than 15 Jira Automation and Power Automate workflows integrated with Microsoft Teams and Slack
  • Developed AI-enabled delivery dashboards to improve visibility into delivery health, blockers and dependencies
  • Automated service-health monitoring using Amazon Q Kiro
  • Supported Jira Cloud and Slack migrations through pillar-level enablement, adoption and ongoing support
  • Partnered with Jira specialists and administration teams to introduce nine Jira Data Center capabilities
  • Contributed to improved Jira governance, workflow standardisation and platform adoption through the Jira Community of Practice
