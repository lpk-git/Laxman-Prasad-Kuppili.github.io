TK-08 Checklist AI

Human-in-the-Loop & AI Guardrails Checklist

Provide a practical checklist for reviewing AI-enabled workflows before allowing AI-generated outputs to influence decisions or actions.

1. WHAT IS THIS?

This is a practical working checklist to verify that human-in-the-loop controls, data privacy, and security guardrails are established before an AI tool goes live.

Note: This is a practical delivery resource and is not a substitute for your organisation's formal security, legal, compliance, or risk policies.

2. THE CHECKLIST

1. PURPOSE

  • Is the AI use case clearly defined?
  • Is AI actually necessary?
  • Is the expected output understood?

2. DATA

  • Do we know what data is being provided?
  • Is sensitive/confidential information appropriately protected?
  • Is unnecessary data excluded?
  • Are data access permissions appropriate?

3. OUTPUT VALIDATION

  • Is AI output validated before critical use?
  • Can users identify uncertain/incorrect output?
  • Is there a fallback when AI cannot provide a reliable result?

4. HUMAN OVERSIGHT

  • Is a human accountable for important decisions?
  • Are approval points defined?
  • Can a human override AI recommendations?
  • Is autonomous action restricted where necessary?

5. SECURITY

  • Are credentials protected?
  • Are tool permissions appropriately scoped?
  • Is least-privilege access applied?
  • Are external actions controlled?

6. TOOL-CONNECTED AI / AGENTS

  • Do we know which tools the AI can access?
  • Are read and write permissions distinguished?
  • Are sensitive actions approval-gated?
  • Can actions be traced/audited?

7. QUALITY

  • Has the solution been tested with realistic scenarios?
  • Have incorrect/hallucinated outputs been considered?
  • Is product/domain context sufficient?

8. MONITORING

  • Can failures be detected?
  • Can AI actions be traced?
  • Is there a mechanism for user feedback?
  • Are unexpected behaviours reviewed?

9. GOVERNANCE

  • Is ownership clear?
  • Are limitations communicated?
  • Are appropriate organisational policies followed?

10. CONTINUOUS REVIEW

  • Are outputs periodically reviewed?
  • Are prompts/configuration updated when required?
  • Are new risks considered as the solution evolves?

3. GO / REVIEW / STOP DECISION

GO

Controls are adequate for the intended low-risk use.

REVIEW

Important controls or validation are incomplete.

STOP

Sensitive/high-impact action lacks appropriate human oversight, security or validation.
