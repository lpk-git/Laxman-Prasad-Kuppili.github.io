TK-02 Checklist Delivery

Sprint Health Check

Provide teams with a lightweight way to inspect whether a sprint is healthy without reducing sprint health to velocity alone.

1. WHAT IS THIS?

This checklist provides a structured way to assess the health of an active sprint based on observed conditions. It is designed to support conversation and early intervention rather than replacing team judgement.

Important: Do not use this checklist to create a simplistic numeric team-performance score or employee rating.

2. THE CHECKLIST

SPRINT GOAL

  • Is there a clear Sprint Goal?
  • Does the team understand it?
  • Is current work aligned with it?

BACKLOG READINESS

  • Are selected items sufficiently understood?
  • Are dependencies visible?
  • Are acceptance expectations clear?

FLOW

  • Is work moving through the workflow?
  • Is WIP becoming excessive?
  • Are items remaining in progress for unusually long periods?
  • Are blockers visible?

QUALITY

  • Is testing happening throughout the sprint?
  • Are defects accumulating?
  • Is work meeting the agreed Definition of Done?

DEPENDENCIES

  • Are external dependencies tracked?
  • Are dependency owners clear?
  • Are unresolved dependencies threatening the Sprint Goal?

CAPACITY

  • Is planned work realistic for available capacity?
  • Have leave/support/operational commitments been considered?

COLLABORATION

  • Are team members collaborating rather than working in silos?
  • Are Product/Engineering/QA aligned?

DELIVERY RISK

  • Is significant work clustering near sprint end?
  • Are there items unlikely to finish?
  • Is scope changing materially?

CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

  • Are previous improvement actions being followed?
  • Is the team learning from recurring blockers?

3. HEALTH INDICATORS

Use the checklist to guide conversation. Synthesize the findings into one of three discussion prompts:

Healthy

Goal is clear, flow is steady, collaboration is high, and risks are actively managed.

Needs Attention

Blockers emerging, WIP increasing, or goal alignment drifting. Intervene early to correct course.

At Risk

Goal is threatened, flow is blocked, or quality is compromising. Requires immediate team response.
