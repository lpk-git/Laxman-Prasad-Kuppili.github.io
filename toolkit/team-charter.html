TK-01 Canvas Delivery

Team Charter Canvas

Provide teams with a practical structure for establishing shared purpose and ways of working.

1. WHAT IS THIS?

The Team Charter Canvas is a foundational tool to help new and existing teams align on why they exist, what they are trying to achieve, and how they agree to interact daily. It replaces assumed behaviors with explicit working agreements.

2. THE CANVAS

1. TEAM PURPOSE

  • Why does this team exist?

2. PRODUCT / VALUE

  • What value are we responsible for delivering?

3. ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Who is responsible for what?

4. CORE VALUES

  • What behaviours matter to us?

5. WORKING AGREEMENTS

  • How do we agree to work together?

6. COMMUNICATION

  • How and when do we communicate?

7. DECISION MAKING

  • How are decisions made?

8. COLLABORATION

  • How do Product, Engineering, QA and other functions collaborate?

9. CONFLICT

  • How will disagreements be handled constructively?

10. DEFINITION OF SUCCESS

  • How will we know the team is effective?

11. CEREMONIES / TEAM RHYTHM

  • What recurring collaboration is useful?

12. CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

  • How will we inspect and improve our ways of working?

3. HOW TO FACILITATE

Keep the session practical and focused on the team's reality rather than presenting abstract agile theory. Use the following simple workshop sequence to populate the canvas:

Purpose
↓
Roles
↓
Values
↓
Working Agreements
↓
Communication
↓
Success
↓
Commitment
